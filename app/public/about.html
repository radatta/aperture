Aperture

A personal life-OS — plan your day, reflect on it.

Aperture is a personal productivity web app, built and used by Rahul Datta, for planning and reflecting on the day.

With your permission, it connects to your Google Calendar to show how your day is shaped and to add events you choose to create. It only touches events you explicitly ask it to.
