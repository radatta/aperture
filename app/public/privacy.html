Privacy Policy

Aperture · effective 2026-09-16

Aperture is a personal app built and used by Rahul Datta. This policy explains what data it accesses and how it is handled.

What Aperture accesses

When you connect a Google account, Aperture accesses your Google account email and your Google Calendar. It reads your calendars to show how your day is shaped, and it creates, edits, or removes calendar events only when you explicitly ask it to.

How it is used

Your data is used solely to provide Aperture's features to you. Calendar data is read on demand and is not retained; Aperture stores only references to the events it created for you (so it can update or remove them). Access tokens are stored encrypted in the operator's private cloud (AWS).

Sharing

Your data is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising.

Google API Limited Use

Aperture's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can disconnect Aperture at any time from your Google Account permissions, which revokes its access to your calendar.
